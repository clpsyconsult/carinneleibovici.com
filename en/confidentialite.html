Privacy policy

Last updated: October 2026.

Data controller

Carinne Leibovici, psychologist. For any question about your data, write to carinne.leibovici@icloud.com.

Data collected

Your identity and contact details, health information you choose to share when getting in touch or during an online session, and browsing data (cookies, IP address, site usage).

Purposes

Answering your enquiries and bookings, providing sessions, improving the site and meeting professional obligations.

Health data

Strictly confidential, except where required by law or in case of serious risk to you or others.

Sharing

Your data is not shared with any third party, except with your explicit consent, where required by law, or at the request of a judicial or administrative authority.

Retention

Contact data is kept for 2 years after our last exchange. Health data is generally kept for 5 years after the last session.

Security

Technical and organisational measures protect your data against loss, alteration or unauthorised access.

Your rights

Under the GDPR, you have the right to access, correct, erase, restrict, object to and port your data. You can exercise these rights by email.

Cookies

You can manage cookies at any time in your browser settings.

Changes

This policy may change. The date of the last update appears at the top of this page.
