About me

Clinical psychologist and psycholinguist, I support children, teenagers and adults with psychological and relationship difficulties.

Education

  • Doctoral studies in psychology, Université Paris 5 Descartes
  • Professional Master's in clinical psychology and psychopathology
  • Research Master's in psycholinguistics, Université Paris 5 Descartes
  • Master's in business management, Université Paris 9 Dauphine

Clinical experience

I spent several years in clinics specialising in developmental and communication disorders in children.

I have been in private practice since 2009. Today all my sessions take place online, with children, teenagers, adults, couples and families.

When needed, I work alongside doctors and other health professionals.

On television

From 2014 to 2018, I took part in the French TV show SOS ma famille a besoin d'aide (NRJ12), which supported families going through hard times.

Coaching
