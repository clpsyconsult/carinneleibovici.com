Your therapist

Carinne Leibovici

I am a clinical psychologist and psycholinguist. For more than fifteen years I have worked with children, teenagers and adults, individually, as couples and as families.

I draw on several approaches, cognitive behavioural, psychodynamic and systemic, chosen to suit your situation. I also offer personal and professional coaching.

All my sessions take place online, so you can attend from wherever you choose.

How it works

From your first message to your first session

  1. You write to me

    Please tell me what brings you, who the sessions are for and what you would like to know before starting.

  2. We set a time

    We agree on the date, time and format: video or audio.

  3. Your first online session

    We get to know each other: you tell me about the issues you are facing, and together we decide on the frequency and length of therapy.
