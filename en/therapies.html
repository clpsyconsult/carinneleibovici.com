Approaches

Several tools, chosen for you

Psychoanalytic therapy

Understanding what is happening unconsciously, to ease symptoms and find new ways of coping.

Cognitive behavioural therapy (CBT)

A short, structured approach: analysing situations, spotting triggers and behaviours, and practical exercises between sessions.

Systemic therapy

Looking at the couple or family as a whole, where each person affects the others, to get communication moving again.

Coaching and development

Personal and professional development, career guidance, skills assessment.
